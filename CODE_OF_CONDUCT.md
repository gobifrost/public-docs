# Code of Conduct

We want a documentation community where people can ask precise questions, correct mistakes, and disagree about technical details without being dismissed or harassed.

Use respectful language. Critique ideas and changes, not people. Do not post personal information, discriminatory language, threats, sexual content, or material you do not have the right to share.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive. For a private report, use the contact information listed on [@jackmusick's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jackmusick).
